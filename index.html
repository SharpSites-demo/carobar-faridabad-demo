04 / Before you visit

Good to know.

Are the cars shown available?

The hero is a concept illustration, not inventory. Ask the dealership for current vehicles, photographs and prices.

Can I arrange a test drive?

Ask the dealership about availability, driving licence requirements and arrangements. No test drive is booked through this preview.

Are finance or warranties included?

No such offer is implied. Ask for written details of any financing, warranty, inspection or transfer arrangements before agreeing.
